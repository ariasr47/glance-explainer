Moving the team wiki to a new host

Chapter 6 of 8, about 1 minute

What happens on switch day, step by step?

Before this: I do each live step, and only after your yes.

  1. Y Send the heads-up. One message, a day before: the date and the new address.
  2. M Take a fresh backup. I check that it opens.
  3. M Make the old wiki read-only. Nobody can edit for about an hour.
  4. M Copy the last changes across. Then I check the pages that matter.
  5. Y+M Point the new address at the new host. You say yes first.
  6. M Forward the old address. Visitors to the old address land on the new one.
If something goes wrong: point the new address back at the old host and turn the forwarding off. The old host still has every page, so the wiki is back within minutes.

Take from this: one evening, six steps, and the way back is one step.

Remember: on switch day the way back is one step, because the old host still has every page.

Chapter 7 of 8, about 1 minute

What could go wrong?

Before this: the way back on switch day is one step.

The old host gets forgotten

High

It still holds every page. Left alone, it turns into the weak spot.

Kept small by: a reminder to switch it off one month after switch day.

Someone misses the message

Medium

They open the old address and wonder where the wiki went.

Kept small by: the old address forwards to the new one for a month.

Sign-in breaks on the new host

Medium

People reach the wiki but cannot log in.

Kept small by: I test sign-in on the spare copy, and again right after the switch.

A page looks different

Low

A few pages use a feature the new host handles in its own way.

Kept small by: I check the twenty most-read pages before and after.

Take from this: the one high risk is about the weeks after the move, so it has a date on the calendar.

Remember: the biggest risk is forgetting the old host, so it gets a reminder.
