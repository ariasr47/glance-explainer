## Ground rules for both lanes

- Layout and rules: `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `AGENTS.md`. Each skill folder is self-contained; skills name files relative to their own `SKILL.md`.
- Design rules that bind: six meaning colours with one meaning each and a glyph or word beside colour; no new colour; no motion; single file; works at 390 px with no sideways scroll; text contrast 4.5:1 in light and dark (the checker computes it from tokens; use existing tokens); touch targets at least 44 px; plain English per `references/writing.md` (no semicolons, no em dashes).
- No new page type. Everything here is components, checker rules and gather-mode changes.
- Zero dependencies, Node 18 built-ins. Browser checks with `py -3` and Playwright, `pw.chromium.launch(channel='chrome')`.
- After any change to the template's `<style>` or `<script>`, paste both blocks into `skills/html-explainer/assets/gallery.html` (byte-identical; check-repo enforces it). Do not touch `docs/index.html` or the examples' blocks; Lane A re-authors `docs/roadmap.html` content only (see A9) and the maintainer re-syncs blocks at merge.
- New rules are warnings unless stated. A new FAIL would make `upgrade.mjs` refuse old pages.
- Japanese: every new interface string goes into `skills/html-explainer/assets/i18n/ui.ja.json` and the script's `T.ja` strings where the script writes text.
- `node tools/check-repo.mjs` exits 0 before each commit. Conventional commits with a scope. No AI attribution trailer. Do not push. Do not edit `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Lane A: roadmap lanes and the backlog view (report changes 1 to 4, 8, 9)

**A1. Lane notes.** A `p.lane-note` right after each lane's `h3` states horizon, confidence and order, as in the boards: "Next 4 weeks. Committed. Top item first." / "1 to 3 months. Likely. Not in order." / "3 months or more. Exploring. Not in order." Checker: warn for each `.lane` without a non-empty `.lane-note`.

**A2. One status vocabulary, derived from the class.** Inside `.lane`, a card's state class prints its word above the title, generated by CSS from the class so it cannot drift (a pseudo-element on the title `b`, shown as its own line, coloured with the state's text colour): `here` "Under way", `wait` "Waits on another", `decide` "Needs your ruling", `stop` "Blocked", `go` "Done", no class "Planned". Japanese words through `:lang(ja)` rules. The glyph dot stays. Checker: warn for a `li.wait` in a lane with neither a `small.after` nor a `small.outside`. Update the gallery's lanes demo, which today uses `wait` for "in progress".

**A3. Exceptions only.** An item less certain than its lane: `data-conf="exploring"` on the `li` plus a `small.conf` with the reason ("Exploring: depends on the platform"); the card and its dot get a dashed border. A real external date: `small.when` beginning with a bold "Fixed date:". Checker: warn for `data-conf` without a non-empty `small.conf`. Fix the specificity of `.lane li span` so the existing `.who` and `.chip` badges render inside lane cards.

**A4. Derived dependencies and a visible landing.**
- The template script counts, for each `.lane li[id]`, the `small.after a[href="#id"]` links on the page and appends `span.unblocks` "Unblocks N" (N at least 1) with an arrow drawn by CSS, as on the boards.
- `small.outside` holds a blocker outside the roadmap ("Outside: Anthropic reviews each listing").
- The in-page link handler (search "every other in-page link scrolls by script") adds a static `is-target` class to the target (removing it from the previous one): a 3 px `--here` outline and a "You jumped here" label, no animation. When the clicked link sits inside an element with an id, the target also gets one `a.jump-back` "Back to: <that element's title>" pointing to it; a later jump removes it.

**A5. Lane budget and folded history.** Checker warnings: a lane with more than 5 live cards (cards without `go`); a `li.go` inside a Now lane ("move shipped work to the shipped fold"). Document two folds below `.lanes`: `details.fold.shipped` (summary "Shipped in X: N items" with a changelog link) and `details.fold.parked` (each item with why and what brings it back). Their summaries carry the ✓ and ✕ glyph dots. A lane header may show "N of 5" (optional `span.lane-count`).

**A6. As-of line.** `p.asof` under the answer bar on a roadmap page: "A plan, not a promise. Items move as we learn." Checker: warn when a `data-type="roadmap"` page has no `.asof`.

**A7. Backlog view (an optional roadmap chapter "What else waits?", not a new type).** From the Backlog board:
- `ol.backlog-top`: at most 5 ranked cards, numbered by CSS, each with the state word (as A2), title, one line and the derived "Unblocks N".
- `div.cutline` with `role="separator"` and a short text ("Cut line. Below is not ranked and not promised").
- `details.theme`, one per theme, summary showing the theme name, "N ideas" and "Top: <first item>", body a compact list.
- `div.parked-shelf` of cards with "Why:" and "Back when:".
- Checker: warn when `ol.backlog-top` has more than 5 items or a page has `details.theme` without a `.cutline` before it.

**A8. Hygiene.** The template script gives each `.lane` an `aria-labelledby` to its `h3` when missing. `references/types.md` roadmap section: the new parts, the backlog chapter, and public-roadmap rules (quarter precision at most, hide internal fields, link shipped items to the changelog). `references/design-system.md`: rows for lane note, state words, exceptions, unblocks, jump landing, folds, as-of, backlog parts. ## Lane B: prioritising that adds up (report changes 5, 6, 7, and the ballot guidance from 9)

**B1. Repair `rank`.**
- Items carry `data-id`; Markdown copy is unchanged in shape, JSON carries ids (`[{id, text}]` or ids alongside text, keep merge backward compatible with old copies that have text only).
- A `button[data-rank-ok]` "This order is right" makes the untouched order a real answer.
- The reason textarea is included in both copies.
- A polite live region announces "<item> moved to 2 of 6".
- Each item gets a "Move to top" button beside up and down; all buttons at least 44 px.
- Checker: warn for rank items without `data-id`.

**B2. Bucket kind.** `fieldset.answer-field[data-kind="buckets"]` with `data-buckets="Now|Next|Later|Not now"` (2 to 5 labels) holds an `ol` of `li[data-id][data-rec]` items (title and one line).
- The script renders one row of bucket buttons per item (`aria-pressed`, the `data-rec` bucket preset and marked ★), then a rank list of the first bucket's items using the B1 control, with "This order is right".
- Copy: Markdown "Now 1: <item>", then one line per other bucket; JSON `{ kind: "buckets", buckets: { "Now": [ids in order], "Next": [...] } }`. A summary line reads "all ★ (recommended)" when nothing changed.
- Without JavaScript the items still read as a list.
- Checker: FAIL for a missing or malformed `data-buckets`, a duplicate `data-id`, or a `data-rec` outside the buckets (a new kind, so no old page breaks). Warn above 15 items.
- Add `data-buckets` to `ATTRS` in `scripts/translate.mjs`.

**B3. Combined ranking in `merge.html`.** For a `rank` question, or a `buckets` question's first bucket, with 3 or more respondents:
- mean place, best and worst place, a spread bar with the mean ring, and a "Contested" flag (amber, with "!") when worst minus best is at least two thirds of the scale (rounded up);
- Kendall's W in words (under 0.3 weak, 0.3 to 0.5 moderate, 0.5 and up strong), and the combined order named only at 0.3 or above;
- items aligned by `data-id`, falling back to text;
- ties broken by best place, then the first respondent's order.
- For `buckets`, also a per-item tally per bucket, flagged "Split" when no bucket has more than half the votes.
- Markdown, CSV and JSON exports include the combined columns.
- Put the arithmetic in one pure function between `/* combine:start */` and `/* combine:end */`. Extend `tools/check-repo.mjs` to extract and run it on a fixture: 4 readers ranking items A to F as A 1,1,3,1 · B 3,2,5,3 · C 2,3,1,2 · D 4,4,4,5 · E 5,5,6,6 · F 6,6,2,4 must give the order A, C, B, D, F, E, W of 0.679 (to 3 decimals), and F alone contested. Wrong arithmetic would ship silently, so this guard earns its place in CI.

**B4. Docs.**
- `references/modes.md` prioritise row: buckets first, then order the top bucket, combined ranking; remove the PARKED note there.
- `SKILL.md` prioritise and ballot sections: a ballot suits 5 to 15 decisions.
- `references/design-system.md` rows: buckets, rank controls.
- Gallery: a buckets field and a rank field in "Ask and collect".
- Japanese strings.

**Lane B tests:**
