## What changes, in one paragraph

Every type opened the same way: two rows of tool chips, the title, a boxed answer and a yellow keep box. The type's own picture only appeared 2 to 4 screens down. In 1.8.0:

- The answer becomes the page's headline.
- The three keep facts sit side by side.
- A new **At a glance** section puts the type's own picture on the first screen.
- On desktop, the rail is the chapter map.
- One **Reading tools** button replaces six.
- A chapter keeps two framing devices instead of four.
- Meaning colour shrinks to dots, glyphs, thin rules and small chips.

Old pages get the new look through `upgrade.mjs`, because the change lives in the template's `<style>` and `<script>` blocks. Old markup must keep working.

## Ground rules (every lane)

- Six meaning colours, each with a glyph or word; no new colour. No motion. One file per page. 390 px with no sideways scroll. 4.5:1 contrast in both themes. 44 px targets. Plain English.
- Keep every existing class and its meaning. Restyle, never rename. Old pages must render well with the new blocks.
- When the template's `<style>` or `<script>` changes, paste both blocks into `skills/html-explainer/assets/gallery.html`. The maintainer re-syncs them at the merge.
- `node tools/check-repo.mjs` exits 0 before each commit. Conventional commits, no attribution trailer, no push.
- No new external requests, no frameworks, Node 18 for scripts.
- Test economy: check each behaviour once in a real browser (Chrome through Playwright is installed: `py -3` has `playwright`) at 390 and 1280 px, light and dark. Add no CI tests.

## Lane 1: the shared change (template sections 3 to 11, and the reader script)

Edit existing rules in place. Do not add new components; lane 2 does that.

1. **Answer.** `p.answer` loses its box and left bar. It is the headline: `--font-head` weight 500, about 28 px desktop and 23 px phone, line height 1.32, `max-width: 34em`. A 56 × 4 px `--here` rule sits above it (a `::before`).
2. **Keep.** `.keep` loses its outer yellow box. Its `strong` becomes a small label: uppercase, letter-spaced, `--ink-faint`. Its `ol` becomes a grid of three yellow cards (`--memory` fill, `--memory-line` border, `--r-comp`). Each card's number is a 26 px navy circle drawn with a counter. The grid stacks under 560 px.
3. **Rail as the chapter map (1100 px and up).**
   - The script enriches each `.rail a` that has a matching `.map a` (same `href`). It adds a `small` with that map item's `span` text and the chapter's minutes, read as the last number in the chapter's `.kicker` (for example "· 2 min"; Japanese "· 約2分").
   - The `.dot` becomes a 26 px numbered circle. Use CSS counters; the recap link shows no number.
   - The current item is a white card with a hairline ring and a filled `--here` circle. Read items show a ✓ circle in `--go`.
   - Below the list: a progress line ("2 of 5 read") with a 6 px bar. The script derives it from the existing done or answered state.
   - `ol.map` is hidden at 1100 px and up, because the rail replaces it there.
4. **Below 1100 px.**
   - The rail is hidden. The sticky chip strip goes; the 4 px `.progress` bar at the top stays.
   - `ol.map` shows as a compact list: one row per chapter with its number circle, the question and its one line, hairlines between rows. No cards.
   - Fix every `scroll-margin-top` that assumed the 125 px sticky strip.
5. **Reading tools.**
   - The script inserts one `button.tools-toggle` ("Reading tools", sliders icon, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`) before `.tools`, and hides `.tools` until it is opened.
   - Opened, `.tools` is a small panel: one 44 px row per tool, the tool's keyboard key on the right where one exists, and Text size as three segments (A− A A+).
   - Escape and a click outside close it.
   - Desktop: the toggle sits in the rail under the progress line. Below 1100 px: the script moves the toggle to the end of `.meta`.
   - Without JavaScript the tools never worked, so hide `.tools` there too.
6. **Chapter header.** `.read-aloud` and the Done `label` become 44 px icon buttons: a speaker and a check, drawn with an inline CSS mask or background SVG.
   - Their text stays in the DOM as the accessible name, visually hidden.
   - The kicker becomes the small uppercase label.
   - Done checked shows the check in `--go`.
7. **Builds on.** `p.from` loses its grey box. It is one quiet line: a small corner-arrow icon, 15 px, `--ink-soft`, with its bold lead kept.
8. **Take.** `.take` loses its left bar. Inside a figure it is the caption under a hairline; under any other component it is a plain line with a hairline above. The bold lead is in `--here`.
9. **Fold.** `details.fold > summary` is a quiet 46 px row with a chevron and hairlines above and below, with no fill. The open body is unchanged.
10. **Remember.** `p.remember` is a `--memory` strip with no border and a small bookmark icon before the bold lead.
11. **Calm colour.**
    - `.split .before` is a white card. `.split .after` is a white card with a 3 px `--go` rule along its top (an inset shadow), not a green fill.
    - `.option[data-rec]` uses `--decide`: border, `--decide-tint` fill, and its chip ("★ " plus `attr(data-rec)`) sits inside the card at the top, not across the border.
    - `.ask .choices a[data-rec]` matches.
    - `.tiles` numbers are `--ink`, with any state shown as a glyph and word under the number.
    - Runbook `p.check` and `p.fail` are quiet lines: a ✓ glyph in `--go`, or a ✕ glyph in `--stop`, then ink text. No filled boxes.
    - `ul.promises` items become cards in an auto-fit grid.
12. **Public pages** (`[data-type="public"]`): no Done control and no progress line in the rail. The reader is a customer.
13. **Print.** Keep the print section working: the tools and toggle hidden, the map shown, chapters unchanged.

## Lane 2: At a glance and the type parts (new template section 9i, new script functions, gallery)

Append a new CSS section `/* ---------- 9i. At a glance and the type parts ---------- */` before `/* ---------- 10.` Add new script functions in one block near the end of the script. Do not edit lane 1's rules.

1. **`section.glance`.**
   - It sits after `.keep` and before `ol.map`, holding `div.glance-head` (an `h2` plus an optional `span.chip` on the right), the type's picture, and a `p.take` (or the picture's own `figcaption.take`).
   - Card style: `--card`, hairline, `--r-chapter`, padding about 22 × 24 px.
   - It is not a chapter: no kicker, no read-aloud, no Done.
   - Skim mode keeps it, and one-chapter mode shows it with the opening.
2. **`div.duo`.** Two columns (`minmax(0, 1.1fr) minmax(0, 1fr)`, gap 20 px) inside a glance or a chapter. It stacks under 760 px. Used by status (health beside timeline), release (tiles beside steps), spec (drawings beside the verdict).
3. **`div.cols`.**
   - Two or three `section.col` columns, each `.go`, `.wait`, `.here`, `.decide` or `.stop`.
   - Each column has an `h3` whose glyph and colour come from the state (✓ go, ! wait, ● here, ◆ decide, ✕ stop), a 3 px top rule in that colour, then a `ul` or `ol`.
   - It stacks under 760 px. Used by handover (Verified, Only claimed, Do first).
4. **`ul.findings`** (research).
   - Each `li[data-strength="strong|moderate|weak"]` holds an `a` or `b` (the finding), a `span` (one line), `small.evidence` ("8 sources") and an optional `small.open` ("2 open").
   - CSS prints the strength word from the attribute and draws three bars, filled 3, 2 or 1.
   - The weak row gets a `--wait-tint` fill, the only tinted row.
   - Grid columns: number, finding, strength, evidence, open. It stacks on a phone.
5. **`ol.layers`** (teach).
   - One `li` per layer, easiest first: a numbered circle, `b` name, `span` line, `span.terms` holding `span` chips for the layer's new words.
   - Exactly one `li.here` gets a `--here` ring and a printed "You are here" label, the same way `.stage[data-here]` does it.
6. **Derived pictures.** The page writes an empty placeholder; the script fills it from parts already on the page, so nothing is typed twice. Without JavaScript the placeholder stays empty and the glance shows its take line.
   - `div.runbook-overview` (runbook). Every `ol.runbook > li`, grouped under its chapter's `h2`, as one row: a checkbox (sharing the step's done state, stored like Done), the step number and the step's first bold sentence. The first unchecked step is marked "Next". Each row links to its step.
   - `div.filmstrip` (tour). One thumbnail per `ol.tour > li`: the step's `img` cloned at 16:10, `object-fit: cover`, its pins, the step number and its `h3`. Each links to the step and gives it an `id` if it has none. The row scrolls sideways inside its box on a phone.
   - `div.rulings` (decision). One row per `.decision`: its `h3` question, the `data-rec` option's `h4` with ★, and a state. The state is "Needs your ruling" (`--decide` glyph) until the matching `fieldset.answer-field` has an answer, then the chosen option. The row for the first unanswered ruling gets the only tint. Each row links to its chapter.
   - `div.qprogress` (gather pages). One dot per `.answer-field[data-q]`, numbered, linking to its field and filled when answered, plus "N of M answered". It updates live.
7. **Gallery.** Add one section per new part: glance, duo, cols, findings, layers, and the four derived pictures, each with a short real-looking neutral example. Use the notes-app, wiki-move or plugin-release topics the gallery already uses.

5. **Checker** (`skills/html-explainer/scripts/check.mjs`).
   - **Warn** when a page whose generator mark is 1.8.0 or later, of any type except roadmap, playbook, field-guide and ballot pages, has no `section.glance`. Older pages are never warned.
   - **Warn** for a glance without a take line.
   - **FAIL:**
     - a `ul.findings li` whose `data-strength` is missing or not strong, moderate or weak
     - an `ol.layers` without exactly one `li.here`
     - a `div.runbook-overview` on a page with no `ol.runbook`
     - a `div.filmstrip` with no `ol.tour`
     - a `div.rulings` with no `.decision`
     - a `div.qprogress` with no `.answer-field[data-q]`
   - The glance's words count toward the reading path and toward no chapter.
   - Check each new rule once against a temp page that should fire and one that should not.
