## F1. The page contract

`<html data-type="field-guide">`. A reference: read once to get oriented, then come back to look things up. The reading path is short. The index sits outside it.

- **Opening:** the answer bar and keep box, as on every page.
- **Three chapters, in this order, each a `section.chapter` with its kicker, question heading, visual and remember line:**
  1. "Where does everything fit?" holds the territory map.
  2. "Which ones cannot wait?" holds the start-here list.
  3. "Look something up" holds the index.
- **Recap:** as usual. No `section.ask`.

## F2. Territory map (`div.territory`)

- **Stages:** `div.stage` holds an `h3` with the stage name. One stage carries `data-here`, which prints a "You are here" label, the same as the 1.6.0 lane "Under way" treatment.
- **Family tiles:** inside a stage, `a.family[href="#fam-<id>"]` holds a `b` name and a one-line `span`.
- **Families no stage owns:** they go in a `div.stage[data-across]` band.
- **Counts and bars come from the script, never from typed text:**
  - The template script counts the entries of the linked family by relevance.
  - It writes "11 now, 1 before launch, 1 later", using words from the index's labels.
  - It also draws a three-part bar, in the board's look: solid `--here`, hatched `--here`, and `--line-strong`.
  - Without JavaScript, the tile shows its name and line only.
- **Key:** a short key under the map names the three bar parts in words.

## F3. Start here (`ol.start-here`)

- At most five items.
- Each item links to an entry id, gives the entry's name and has one "why" line.

## F4. Index (`div.index`)

- **Labels:** `data-rel-labels="Now|Before launch|Later"` sets the three relevance words. The values are `now`, `next` and `later`, and the labels default to those words.
- **Toolbar:** `div.guide-tools`. The script adds a search `input` with a visible label, and filter buttons for each relevance plus All. Each button shows a live count.
- **Default filter:** the first relevance (`now`). A status line says "Showing X of Y", plus the search text when there is one.
- **Families:** each is a `details.family-index[id="fam-<id>"]` with a `summary` that holds an `h3` and the derived counts. Optional `h4.group` subheadings sit inside.
- **Entries:**
  - Each is a `div.entry[id][data-rel]`.
  - Its row holds `b.name`, the relevance word (generated from `data-rel` and the labels, with the glyphs ● ◐ ○) and `p.def`.
  - Its `div.entry-body` holds `p.where` (bold lead, for example "In the notes app:") and an optional `p.see` with links to other entry ids.
  - The script makes the row a button with `aria-expanded`. It adds a "Copy link to this field" button to each open entry, copying the URL with its hash.
- **Search:** it matches name, definition and where-line, and is case-insensitive. A family with no matches shows "Nothing here matches" and how many are hidden.
- **Deep links:** a link to an entry id, or a page opened with that hash, opens the entry. It also opens its family, lifts a filter that would hide it, and scrolls to it. Use 1.6.0's link handler and its `is-target` landing, and do not write a second handler.
- **Print and skim:** printing shows every entry open. Skim mode keeps rows and hides entry bodies.

## F5. Checker (`data-type="field-guide"`)

- **FAIL:**
  - no `.territory`, no `.index`, or no `.start-here`
  - an entry without an id, `b.name`, `p.def` or `p.where`
  - a `data-rel` outside `now`, `next` and `later`
  - duplicate ids
  - a `section.ask`
  - an `a.family` whose target family does not exist
  - a start-here link whose target entry does not exist
- **Warn:**
  - a definition over 20 words
  - more than 150 entries
  - more than 12 families
  - more than 5 start-here items
  - a family tile with digits in its text, because counts are derived
- **Reading path:** exclude everything inside `.index` from the reading-path word count and the chapter word limits. The usual limits then apply to the rest.
